Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about buying, booking and investing with iDream.

Home/FAQs

What is iDream Groups?
iDream Groups is a dynamic business conglomerate spanning realty & properties, cinemas, and sports — as proud owners of the iDream Tiruppur Tamizhans in the TNPL league. In real estate, we develop customer-centric residential plots and homes across Tamil Nadu.
Where are iDream projects located?
Our ongoing and upcoming projects are located in prime, well-connected neighbourhoods across Chennai (Tondiarpet, Royapuram) and Madurai (Mattuthavani), chosen for connectivity, infrastructure, and long-term value.
What types of properties does iDream offer?
We offer DTCP-approved residential plots, premium villa plots, and ready-to-move apartments — designed for families, end-users, and investors alike.
Are iDream projects DTCP & RERA approved?
Yes. Our developments carry the necessary DTCP and RERA approvals, with clear titles and transparent documentation, so you can invest with complete peace of mind.
How do I book a plot or home with iDream?
Simply enquire through our contact form or call us. Our team will share the brochure, arrange a site visit, and guide you through booking, documentation, and registration end to end.
Does iDream assist with home loans and EMI options?
Absolutely. We partner with leading banks and financial institutions to help you secure home loans and flexible EMI plans, and our team assists with the entire approval process.
What sets iDream apart from other developers?
Beyond clear titles and approvals, we focus on fast-developing locations, superior construction quality, on-time delivery, and honest pricing — backed by the strength and credibility of the larger iDream Groups brand.
Can I schedule a site visit before booking?
Yes. We encourage every customer to visit the site. Just enquire and our team will arrange a convenient visit, walk you through the layout and amenities, and answer all your questions on the spot.
What documents will I receive on purchase?
You receive the registered sale deed, approved layout plan, parent documents, encumbrance certificate, and all statutory approvals — ensuring a clean, transparent transaction.
Does iDream offer ready-to-move-in homes?
Yes. Alongside plotted developments, projects such as iDream Apoorva offer ready and near-completion homes, so you can move in or invest with minimal waiting.
What amenities are included in iDream projects?
Our communities are planned with wide internal roads, landscaped greenery, street lighting, water and drainage provisions, and gated security — with premium amenities varying by project.
How can I track my project's construction progress?
Our relationship team shares regular updates and milestone photos, and you're always welcome to visit the site. For any query, our support line is just a call away.

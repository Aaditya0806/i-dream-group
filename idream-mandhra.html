iDream Mandhra is a premium, CMDA & RERA-approved plotted development at Tollgate, Tondiarpet — one of North Chennai's fastest-developing corridors. Spread across 61 thoughtfully planned plots, it is designed for secure, comfortable and family-friendly living.

With clear titles, strong infrastructure and excellent connectivity to major roads and city landmarks, iDream Mandhra offers the perfect opportunity to build your dream home while ensuring great future value for your investment.

  • Just minutes from Thiruvottiyur High Road and North Terminus Road.
  • Close to schools, hospitals, temples and iDream Cinemas.
  • Easy access to Chennai Central and the beach (approx. 900 m away).

Legal Assurance

iDream Mandhra is a CMDA & RERA-approved layout with proper documentation from mother deed to patta, verified by experienced legal experts. This ensures a 100% secure and hassle-free property purchase process.

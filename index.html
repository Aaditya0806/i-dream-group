We're Here to Help

Your questions, our honest answers

What is iDream Groups?
iDream Groups is Tamil Nadu's most trusted conglomerate — spanning real estate, cinemas, and cricket. With 18+ years of reliable service, 32+ successful projects, and 4,500+ happy families, we are one of the most authentic and customer-friendly builders in the region.
Where are iDream projects located?
Our projects are in the heart of your neighbourhood — prime, well-connected locations across Chennai (Tondiarpet, Royapuram) and Madurai (Mattuthavani). Every site is handpicked for its connectivity, reliable infrastructure, and long-term value so your family's investment is always safe.
What types of properties does iDream offer?
We offer 100% verified DTCP-approved residential plots, premium villa plots, and ready-to-move homes — trusted by thousands of satisfied families, first-time buyers, and smart investors across Tamil Nadu.
Are iDream projects DTCP & RERA approved?
Absolutely — every iDream project is 100% authentic with verified DTCP and RERA approvals, clear titles, and fully transparent documentation. We believe honest dealings build lasting relationships, so you can invest with complete peace of mind.
How do I book a plot or home with iDream?
It's simple and friendly! Just fill the enquiry form or give us a call. Our warm, helpful team will share the brochure, arrange a free site visit at your convenience, and walk you through booking, documentation, and registration — hand-holding you every step of the way.
Does iDream assist with home loans and EMI options?
Yes! We've partnered with all leading banks to make your dream home affordable. Our friendly team handles the entire loan approval process for you — from paperwork to disbursement — so you can focus on planning your new life, not stressing over finances.
What sets iDream apart from other developers?
Trust, reliability, and happy families — that's what sets us apart. 18+ years of on-time completion, 100% authentic approvals, honest pricing with no hidden costs, and a genuinely friendly team that treats every customer like family. Our 4,500+ satisfied homeowners are our biggest proof.
Can I schedule a site visit before booking?
Of course — we actually encourage it! Our friendly team will arrange a visit at a time that works for you, personally walk you through the layout, show you the amenities, and honestly answer every question on the spot. No pressure, no rush — just a warm, transparent experience.
What documents will I receive on purchase?
You receive the complete, verified set — registered sale deed, approved layout plan, parent documents, encumbrance certificate, and all statutory approvals. Everything is 100% authentic and transparent, because we believe you deserve total clarity on the biggest investment of your life.
Does iDream offer ready-to-move-in homes?
Yes! Projects like iDream Apoorva offer ready-to-move and near-completion homes. No waiting, no uncertainty — just pick up the keys and start your happy new chapter with your family.
What amenities are included in iDream projects?
Every iDream community is thoughtfully planned for comfortable, happy living — wide roads, lush greenery, reliable street lighting, proper water & drainage, and gated security. We build neighbourhoods where families feel safe, connected, and truly at home.
How can I track my project's construction progress?
Our dedicated, friendly relationship team shares regular updates and milestone photos so you're never in the dark. You're always welcome to visit the site in person, and for any query — big or small — our support team is just one call away. That's the iDream promise.

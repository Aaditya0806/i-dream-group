Privacy Policy

How iDream Properties collects, uses and protects the information you share with us.

Home/Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how iDream Properties ("we", "us", "our") handles the personal information you choose to share with us through this website.

Information We Collect

When you voluntarily send us electronic mail or fill up the form, we will keep a record of this information so that we can respond to you. We only collect information from you when you register on our site or fill out a form. Also, when filling out a form on our site, you may be asked to enter your: name, e-mail address or phone number. You may, however, visit our site anonymously.

How We Use Your Information & Consent

In case you have submitted your personal information and contact details, we reserve the rights to Call, SMS, Email or WhatsApp about our products and offers, even if your number has DND activated on it.

By submitting an enquiry form on this website and ticking the consent box, you authorize iDream Properties & its representatives to contact you with updates and notifications via Email / SMS / WhatsApp / Call, and you agree that this authorization overrides any DND / NDNC registration on your number.

Why We Collect This Information

We use the details you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, share information about our plots, apartments, commercial spaces and offers, schedule site visits, and provide the services you have requested. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Keeping Your Information Secure

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us against unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse. Access to your details is limited to authorized team members who need it to respond to you.

Your Choices

You may withdraw your consent or ask us to stop contacting you at any time by writing to us using the contact details below. On receiving your request, we will update our records accordingly.
